# 🌊 Under the Memory

## 📖 Overview

**Under the Memory** is an immersive VR experience that reconstructs Bristol Harbourside using **3D Gaussian Splatting**, combining photorealistic spatial capture with interactive environmental storytelling.

The experience presents Bristol across different points in time, contrasting memories of the present-day Harbourside with a future affected by long-term sea-level rise. Players explore the environment, uncover traces of the past, and interact with environmental clues that reveal how the city and its ecosystem have changed.

The project explores how immersive technologies can communicate environmental change through place, memory, and interaction rather than presenting information through traditional visualisation alone.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🌆 3D Gaussian Splatting Environment

- **Photorealistic Reconstruction:** Uses 3D Gaussian Splatting to represent areas of Bristol Harbourside within VR
- **Spatial Memory:** Captured environments are used as representations of places and moments from the present day
- **Environmental Transformation:** Contrasts familiar urban spaces with a future affected by rising sea levels

### 🕰️ Memory & Time-Based Storytelling

- **Memory Interaction:** Players can access representations of the Harbourside from an earlier point in time
- **Archive Lens:** An interactive tool allows players to investigate environmental clues and observe changes associated with them
- **Past and Future Contrast:** The experience uses changes in the environment to communicate the passage of time and environmental loss

### 🔍 Environmental Exploration

- **Interactive Clues:** Players discover and investigate traces connected to places, species, and environmental change
- **Environmental Storytelling:** Narrative information is communicated through the environment and player interaction
- **Guided Exploration:** Interactive elements encourage players to observe how different parts of the environment have changed

### 🌊 Underwater Environment

- **Submerged Future:** The experience transitions from the city environment into an underwater setting
- **Ecosystem Exploration:** Players investigate environmental and ecological elements within the submerged world
- **Interactive Objectives:** Environmental interactions guide players through the later stages of the experience

### 🥽 Immersive VR Interaction

- **VR-Based Exploration:** Players physically explore and investigate the reconstructed environment
- **Interactive Objects:** Environmental elements and tools respond to player interaction
- **Hand & Controller Interaction:** VR interactions are designed around natural player movement and object manipulation

## 🛠️ Technical Specifications

- **Game Engine:** Unity 6.0 (`6000.0.59f2`)
- **Programming Language:** C#
- **XR Standard:** OpenXR
- **Interaction Framework:** XR Interaction Toolkit
- **Target Headset:** Meta Quest
- **Core Visual Technology:** 3D Gaussian Splatting
- **Rendering Pipeline:** Universal Render Pipeline (URP)
- **Platform:** Android / Meta Quest
- **3D & Environment Tools:** RealityScan and Autodesk Maya
- **Core Systems:** Gaussian Splat rendering, environmental interactions, time-based scene transitions, VR object interaction, and narrative-driven exploration
